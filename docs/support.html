Common questions

Is my data uploaded? No. cURL is parsed on your device and saved requests stay in local browser storage.

Why was a request not sent? apiVU needs permission for the target host. Allow it when prompted, then try again.

My cookies are not applied. Chrome does not allow a manual Cookie header in Fetch. Use the browser-managed cookies option instead.

A cURL option is ignored. Unsupported options and shell syntax are not interpreted.
